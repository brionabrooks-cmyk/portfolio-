Agentforce Global Launch: A Three-Theater Demand Engine

Global GTM Demand Generation Cross-Regional Leadership AI Product Launch

Salesforce's flagship AI product needed immediate, Sales-prioritized pipeline across three international markets at once — with no single marketing org owning all three. I built the global strategy, aligned regional Sales and Marketing leadership across EMEA, LATAM, and APAC around shared pipeline targets, and created a high-velocity program infrastructure that generated 8-figure pipeline in six months and became the repeatable standard for subsequent product launches.

The Problem

Agentforce was Salesforce's highest-priority product launch — but the commercial challenge wasn't awareness, it was Sales-ready pipeline at speed across markets with different buyer dynamics, languages, and Sales team priorities. EMEA, LATAM, and APAC all needed to move simultaneously without a unified regional authority to coordinate them.

Strategic Approach

The 9-webinar-per-month demand engine served as the backbone, with regionally adapted paid, nurture, and field layers built around it.

What It Produced

8-figure incremental pipeline in the first six months. The frameworks I built were adopted across all three regions as repeatable playbooks, reducing time-to-market for subsequent launches. The broader demand program — running throughout my Salesforce tenure — contributed 9-figure pipeline. Regional teams continued running the architecture after the launch window closed.

Intuit Mailchimp · B2B Marketing · March 2026

DTC 2026: Reinsert Mailchimp Into the Expert Conversation

Event-Led Demand Pipeline Strategy Competitive Positioning Cross-Functional Execution

Klaviyo owned the ecommerce mindshare conversation. Mailchimp had the product to compete — but no presence in the rooms where DTC operators form opinions. I diagnosed the positioning gap, aligned Sales and Content teams around a shared program strategy, and built a high-credibility expert event that exceeded its stretch pipeline goal and created a durable on-demand asset infrastructure extending well beyond the launch day.

The Problem

The challenge wasn't product — it was presence and trust among the DTC operators who influence buying decisions. Klaviyo had embedded itself in that community. Mailchimp needed a program that didn't just reach DTC buyers but earned credibility with them through voices they already trusted. Pure demand capture wouldn't work; this required a brand-and-pipeline hybrid strategy.

Strategic Approach

I aligned Product Marketing, Sales, and Content teams around a single program frame, then designed the event around third-party credibility at scale — sourcing and managing 7 DTC operators and investors with 400K+ combined follower reach and $500M+ in DTC revenue managed. Promotion ran across email, Sales outreach sequences, organic social, and panelist networks — with post-event architecture built from day one: on-demand seeding into nurture, YouTube, and quarterly newsletters.

What It Produced

1,210 registrants vs. 1,000 stretch goal. 118 leads routed to Sales — 114 net new. 1 in 4 live attendees became a sales-qualified lead. 51% of registrants were paid accounts, validating ICP targeting. 264 free-to-paid upgrade targets entered the nurture pipeline. The event format and framework were positioned for repeat use in subsequent quarters.

Intuit Mailchimp · FY26 Q2 · Jan–Feb 2026

ABM Sales Play #3: Competitive Conquest at Account Precision

ABM Paid Social Competitive Strategy Messaging Architecture

Converting high-value Klaviyo customers requires more than a better price — it requires a message that meets them where their dissatisfaction lives. I led the strategic brief, messaging architecture, and cross-functional alignment that turned a January deadline into a live competitive playbook across Meta and LinkedIn — one modular enough to scale into Q3 and beyond.

The Problem

Mailchimp needed to convert Shopify merchants earning $750+ MRR away from Klaviyo — a segment with high lifetime value and strong platform loyalty. The challenge was message precision at account level: switching messaging that didn't feel generic, paired with ecommerce proof points that validated Mailchimp's product credibility with skeptical power users.

Strategic Approach

I developed a two-track paid social brief requiring tight alignment between Creative, Channel, and Sales teams: a DSB ecommerce track (Shopify integration ROI, automation proof) running in parallel with a Klaviyo switcher conquest track ("Break Up With Klaviyo," 42% cost savings). LinkedIn used personalization macros for account-level relevance; Meta led with testimonial-anchored formats. The brief was designed as a modular system — reusable copy and asset structures — not a one-time campaign.

What It Produced

The January 14 launch hit on schedule across both channels. 22 assets and 64+ copy lines delivered, with the creative framework adopted as a reusable switcher playbook carried into Q3 planning. The program established a structured competitive conquest model that Sales and Creative could brief against without starting from zero.
